Completion status

BM has an internally established foundation and additional scoped results. The finite Seed theorems do not resolve the six broad families: unrestricted nonlinear Jordan optima; global regular realization and augmentation; constrained or infinite causal realization; general quantum recovery implementation and constants; nonlinear optimal repair beyond solved classes; and further infinite-dimensional native theories.

Independent proof review, substantive dependency auditing, historical source recovery and demonstrated practical benefit remain separate evidence requirements. Unspecified contracts and failed reductions stay explicit.
