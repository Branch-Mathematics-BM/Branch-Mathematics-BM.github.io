About this site

A public technical reference for Branch Mathematics, GURF, and associated research programs.

The site is mathematics-first. It presents definitions, theorem identifiers, equations, failure modes, and research status rather than conversation history. Current foundations are versioned, open problems remain open, and imported classical mathematics is not relabeled as a new discovery merely because BM gives it a new role.
