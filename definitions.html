Formal definitions

Definitions

Core definitions are receiver- and domain-aware. The short entries below are a public reference; the canonical theorem sources govern edge cases.

Branch specification

\[B=(S_B,\tau_B,X_B,A_B,\mathcal L_B,\mathcal O_B,\mathcal P_B).\]

Sector, type, carrier, admissible objects, laws, operations, and contracts/passports in context.

Task equivalence

\[x\sim_\mathscr Qx'\iff T(x)=T(x')\ \forall T\in\mathscr Q.\]

The coarsest exact task quotient identifies exactly the distinctions irrelevant to every declared task.

Root

An exported Response is a Root only relative to a particular receiving Passport. A Root can fail after re-entry into a stronger Branch.

Realization family

The family of interpretations/configurations satisfying the full signature, constraints, and context. Separate local feasibility does not guarantee a global member exists.
