Branch Mathematics

Branch Mathematics (BM)

Canonical framework definition

Branch Mathematics is meta-structural mathematics of lawful representation and transformation.

Branch

Core structural object

A Branch is a typed mathematical carrier of information, distinctions, structure, and lawful mathematical behavior.

Interaction

Core structural object

An Interaction is a lawful joint mathematical action or relation between participating Branches.

Leaf

Core structural object

A Leaf is mathematical information extracted from an interaction.

Tree

Core structural object

A Tree is an organized structure of Branches and Interactions that produces a mathematical response.

Response

Core structural object

A Response is a law-valued output produced by a mathematical Tree or associated lawful structure.

Root

Active structural definition

A Root is a mathematical response or resulting structure that can participate in further lawful mathematical response, attachment, or rebranching.

Passport

Active structural definition

A Passport specifies distinctions, observations, constraints, admissibility conditions, compatibility requirements, or other information required for lawful passage across a mathematical interface.

Structural Operations

Representation

The lawful expression of mathematical information within a specified Branch, structure, or interface.

Transformation

A lawful change from one mathematical representation or structure to another under stated admissibility requirements.

Composition

The lawful combination of compatible mathematical operations, Branches, interfaces, or responses.

Realization

The lawful construction or instantiation of a mathematical object, representation, or response satisfying its required structure.

Re-entry

The lawful return of a transformed, exported, or derived mathematical object into a compatible mathematical structure for further use.

GURF

GURF

Framework definition

GURF is a mathematical framework for lawful system responses.

Information Sufficiency

Information is sufficient for a task when the distinctions preserved by the available representation are enough to support the required lawful response or continuation.

Obstruction

An obstruction is a missing distinction, incompatible constraint, or structural failure that prevents a required lawful continuation.

Repair

A repair is additional information or lawful structural modification sufficient to remove an identified obstruction.

Continuation

Continuation is the ability of a mathematical or system representation to proceed lawfully into the response required by a task.

Definition Status

Definitions in this encyclopedia may be refined as the formal mathematics develops. Canonical definitions are distinguished from active working definitions when necessary.

Revisions are recorded in the mathematical changelog rather than through conversational development history.
