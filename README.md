# Branch Mathematics Research Encyclopedia

**Branch Mathematics (BM)** is a mathematical metaframework for carrying one mathematical problem through multiple distinct lawful mathematical Branches while preserving the native mathematics of each Branch and governing their lawful representation, interaction, transformation, composition, comparison, realization, computation, export, re-entry, and further mathematical development.

The public corpus also contains **GURF**, the response-specialized framework under BM for problems involving Response, Observation, Tasks, Interventions, ambiguity, reconstruction, continuation, uncertainty, constraints, and response sufficiency.

# Branch Mathematics

A compact descriptive identity is:

> **BM is meta-structural mathematics of lawful representation and transformation.**

The governing principle is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{one problem}
\to
\text{multiple lawful mathematical focuses}
\to
\text{lawful interaction and combined computation}

BM does not replace native mathematics.

A PDE remains a PDE.

A manifold remains a manifold.

A graph remains a graph.

A Markov kernel remains a Markov kernel.

Native mathematics determines truth inside those structures.

BM governs how such structures may participate lawfully in one larger mathematical problem.

# Contract and Passport

\[
\boxed{

\[
\boxed{

Passport applicability and Passport acceptance are distinct:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Acc}(\mathcal P)
\subseteq

}

Cases:

1. \(x\notin D_{\mathcal P}\): Passport does not apply.
2. \(x\in D_{\mathcal P}\setminus\operatorname{Acc}(\mathcal P)\): Passport applies and rejects.
3. \(x\in\operatorname{Acc}(\mathcal P)\): Passport applies and accepts.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{not applicable}
\neq
\text{applicable but false}.
}
