Status discipline

Research claims are not all given the same status

The site separates source-certified foundation, internally verified developments, classical imports, synthesis, candidate results, counterexamples, repairs, and open problems.

SC

Source-certified

Part of the canonical source material and carried into the frozen Version 1 foundation.

RP / CH-V

Review-proved / hardened

Proved or strengthened during internal review and verification, without implying independent peer review.

CI

Classical / imported

Established mathematics used inside BM and credited as such.

BM-SYN

BM synthesis

Placement or synthesis of known ingredients inside the BM task-and-distinction architecture.

CH-C

Candidate

Active development that has not yet been promoted to the canonical foundation.

FW / OPEN

Firewalls and open problems

Counterexamples that block overclaiming, plus explicitly unresolved research questions.

Open frontier

Current hard questions

The most useful research targets are the points where exact structure, approximation, realization, and physical admissibility still fail to line up cleanly.

Nonlinear finite-ε realization Characterize the true optimum for nonlinear continuous trajectory compression below the exact Jordan obstruction threshold.
Dimension-aware quantum stability Relate approximate algebraic commutator defect to physically realizable CPTP recovery under explicit finite-dimensional assumptions.
Global smooth realization Determine when local factorization or repair data can be assembled into a global smooth low-dimensional realization.
Approximate causal compatibility Understand when independently optimal local covers can be made dynamically compatible without large global surcharge.
Semantic phase transitions Quantify when weak coupling creates new task-relevant distinctions and how positive tolerance delays those transitions.
Scalable interface synthesis Find tractable classes where minimal composable interfaces can be computed without combinatorial explosion.
