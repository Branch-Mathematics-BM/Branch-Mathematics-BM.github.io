The six labels

LabelMeaningBoundary
DefinedAn explicit mathematical object/type, contract or convention is stated.Does not prove nonempty realization, physical reality or an independently calibrated native instance.
TheoremA scoped result/counterexample has a written source proof, standard derivation or explicit accepted certificate.All stated hypotheses remain necessary. This audit does not convert internal proof evidence into independent validation or novelty.
Conditional theoremAn implication is stated, but its native bridge, quantitative estimates or structured-descent assumptions are unestablished for the intended application.Do not claim the consequent for that application until those obligations pass.
HypothesisA physical proposal, active theorem candidate, unresolved research question or reported result without recovered full proof.A source gap is identified as a source gap, rather than proof that the original result is false.
Physical interpretationA semantic/physical reading or translation of existing mathematics.Requires extra physics and testable consequences to become a new physical law.
Missing equationA demanded universal or iterative physical model lacks its governing quantitative/constitutive equation.A missing proof or an ordinary unspecified application parameter is not automatically a missing fundamental equation.

Lifecycle is recorded separately. Counterexamples and impossibility theorems can carry the Theorem label; a retired proposal stays retired.
