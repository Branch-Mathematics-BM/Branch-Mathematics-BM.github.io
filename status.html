Status & Verification

Every public claim should advertise what kind of statement it is and what has not yet been established.

StatusMeaning
ProvedWritten argument under stated hypotheses in the internal corpus; not a claim of independent peer review or historical novelty.
ConditionalA valid implication or engine whose hypotheses include an unproved/unverified application-specific estimate.
Conjecture / openA research target, interpretation, or physical hypothesis not yet established as theorem.
FailureA counterexample, no-go, broken certificate, or route that must remain visible.
