1. Purpose of the Changelog

The changelog records changes to the mathematics and its formal public presentation.

It is not a conversation archive.

The record is intended to answer questions such as:

What definition changed? · Which theorem was corrected? · What assumption was added? · Which result was promoted or downgraded? · What failed? · Which equations were generalized? · What research status changed? · What public reference page was added or expanded?

2. Revision Principles

\[ \boxed{ \text{correction} \neq \text{erasure} } \]

When mathematics changes, the preferred procedure is to record:

previous form · reason for revision · corrected form · consequence for dependent results · new status

This allows the research history to remain mathematically auditable.

6. September 3, 2026 — Passport Mathematics

September 3, 2026
PASSPORT FORMALIZATION

Passport mathematics expanded into a dedicated formal reference

A general working Passport representation was introduced as

\[ P = \left( X, \mathcal C, \{b_c\}_{c\in\mathcal C}, \mathcal A \right). \]

Raw Passport equivalence is

\[ \boxed{ x\sim_Px' \Longleftrightarrow b_c(x)=b_c(x') \quad \forall c\in\mathcal C. } \]

The page now distinguishes raw equivalence from operation-compatible structured quotienting.

12. BM-SQ-01 — Structured Quotient Result

BM-SQ-01 PROVED FINITARY ALGEBRA

The finitary algebraic structured-quotient result was recorded under its approved project identifier:

\[ \boxed{ x_i\sim_P y_i \quad\forall i \Longrightarrow \sigma(x_1,\ldots,x_n) \sim_P \sigma(y_1,\ldots,y_n). } \]

This is the condition required for

\[ \overline{\sigma} ([x_1]_P,\ldots,[x_n]_P) = [\sigma(x_1,\ldots,x_n)]_P \]

to be well-defined.

Raw Passport equivalence alone is therefore insufficient for a structured algebraic quotient.

13. Structured Quotient Counterexample Preserved

COUNTEREXAMPLE

The coordinate-projection equivalence on

\[ X=\mathbb R^2 \]

was retained as an explicit example showing that a valid equivalence relation need not be compatible with every operation.

Under

\[ (x_1,x_2)\sim_P(y_1,y_2) \Longleftrightarrow x_1=y_1, \]

coordinate swapping fails to preserve the equivalence.

19. Yang–Mills Variational Correction

CORRECTION YANG–MILLS

Variational reasoning was corrected to distinguish monotonicity of ordered Ritz approximations from behavior of their differences.

Spectral-gap differences are not automatically variationally monotone merely because individual ordered Ritz values possess variational bounds.

Any gap argument therefore requires separate error certification.

20. Stochastic Semantics Firewall

CORRECTION STOCHASTIC

Stochastic transformations are no longer treated as though ordinary deterministic equivalence automatically applies.

A Markov kernel is represented as

\[ M: X \longrightarrow \mathcal P(Y), \]

with test-function action

\[ M^\dagger\varphi(x) = \mathbb E[\varphi(Y)\mid X=x]. \]

Measurability and probability-law semantics must remain explicit.

21. Nondeterministic Quantifier Firewall

CORRECTION NONDETERMINISTIC

Existential and universal nondeterministic semantics were formally separated:

\[ \Diamond_R(U) = \{x:\exists y\in U,\ xRy\}, \] \[ \Box_R(U) = \{x:\forall y,\ xRy\Rightarrow y\in U\}. \]

These operators are not interchangeable.

22. Joint Feasibility Firewall

CORRECTION QUANTIFIERS

Separate feasibility was explicitly distinguished from common-witness feasibility.

\[ \forall c\;\exists x_c \]

does not imply

\[ \exists x\;\forall c. \]

Joint feasibility requires a condition such as

\[ \boxed{ \bigcap_{c\in\mathcal C} P_c \neq \varnothing. } \]

23. Differential Privacy Representation Correction

CORRECTION PRIVACY

Differential privacy was separated from ordinary exact equivalence semantics.

Privacy requires comparison of probability laws using appropriate divergences, likelihood bounds, or other quantitative structures rather than exact equality alone.

29. BM and GURF Placement Clarified

GOVERNANCE

BM is the project-level meta-structural mathematics.

GURF is invoked when response semantics are genuinely present.

\[ \boxed{ \text{BM governs structure;} \qquad \text{GURF specializes response.} } \]

Neither framework needs to be invoked when ordinary domain mathematics already answers the problem without additional structural leverage.
