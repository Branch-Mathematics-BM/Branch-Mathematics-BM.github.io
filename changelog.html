Purpose

This changelog records the evolution of the mathematics itself.

It documents changes to definitions, equations, theorems, structures, assumptions, proofs, classifications, and research status.

The changelog is not a conversation archive. It records only changes that are relevant to the mathematical research corpus.

The Branch Mathematics public research encyclopedia was established as the primary public reference for Branch Mathematics, GURF, associated research, mathematical definitions, and formal results.

September 2026

Branch Mathematics Public Definition

Branch Mathematics is presented as meta-structural mathematics of lawful representation and transformation.

Its public structure emphasizes distinct lawful mathematical branches, interactions, combined computation, and resulting mathematical responses while preserving the mathematics of each participating structure.

September 2026

GURF Placement Clarified

GURF is presented as a mathematical framework for lawful system responses and as a specialization of the broader Branch Mathematics structure when genuine response semantics are present.

September 2026

Research Status Separation

Mathematical validity, direct derivation, external novelty, physical interpretation, experimental evidence, and independent external validation are treated as separate research questions.

Revision Policy

Earlier mathematical forms may be corrected, generalized, restricted, superseded, or rejected when later analysis requires it.

The current canonical mathematical form remains the primary reference. Earlier versions are retained only when they are useful for understanding a mathematically significant change.
