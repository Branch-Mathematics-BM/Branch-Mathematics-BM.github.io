Steadman Hypothesis

A three-part research hypothesis: Relativistic-Determination (RD), Smoothing, and Efficiency. The present state is structural and conditional—not a new universal physical law.

Hypothesis

Relativistic-Determination

RD is intended to track physically relevant distinctions/relations that remain present somewhere in a complete lawful description even when no longer visible to one receiver, subsystem, scale, or coarse representation.

Central hypothesis: RD cannot be destroyed; it can only be redistributed.

Hypothesis

Smoothing

A process by which accessible/local structure becomes more regular or less fine-grained while distinctions may move into other scales, correlations, carriers, or completion fibres.

Principle

Efficiency

Within a fixed Physical Transmission Domain, compare actual useful transmitted response with the ideal admissible transmitted response.

\[\eta_R=\frac{R_{\rm useful}}{R_{\rm ideal}},\qquad R_{\rm out}=\eta_RR_{\rm ideal}.\]

V1.1 vacuum-Hessian addendum

The freeze includes the exact ground-state identity for H=-cΔ+V, HΩ=E₀Ω, W=-2\log Ω:

\[\frac c2\Delta W-\frac c4|\nabla W|^2+V=E_0,\qquad \Omega^{-1}(H-E_0)\Omega=-c(\Delta-\nabla W\cdot\nabla).\]

This is an exact mathematical identity and fixes normalization; it does not by itself establish a Yang–Mills mass gap.

What is mathematically supported

Current support includes injective transport of complete relations, uniform-contraction and quadratic-form gap criteria, a conditional Yang–Mills block engine, mixed-Hessian response estimates, boundary-score refinements, energy-metric requirements under smoothing, completion-fibre accounting, and a small-branch continuation lemma.

Permanent negative record: no universal fixed-receiver RD scalar conservation has been proved; Smoothing + persistence does not imply a mass gap; local positivity does not exclude collective soft modes; finite Schur pivots do not guarantee an infinite-dimensional margin; Planck stepping is not a gap proof; and Efficiency alone does not derive electroweak vacuum data.
