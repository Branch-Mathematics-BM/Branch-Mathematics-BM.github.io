The declared physical model

The starting dynamics are the standard Kogut–Susskind lattice Hamiltonian in the project's stated normalization. Steadman terminology does not supply an additional force or a newly derived Hamiltonian.

\[H=c_Kh,\qquad h=K-\beta T,\qquad K=-\sum_{e\in E}\Delta_e,\qquad T=\sum_{p\in\mathcal P}\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}U_p.\]

SU(2) links use the unit-three-sphere metric: representation \(n=2j\) has link electric energy \(n(n+2)\). Gauge invariance is imposed at every vertex, including boundary vertices, with no external charges. The cube retains its shared-link constraints; its six faces are not six independent rotators.

How omitted states enter the certificate

Let \(\Pi=\mathbf1_{[0,24)}(K)\) on the physical Hilbert space and \(Q=I-\Pi\). Write \(A=\Pi h\Pi\), \(B=Qh\Pi\), and \(C=QhQ\). The finite calculation retains both the interaction matrix and the exact omitted-coupling Gram matrix:

\[W_{ij}=\langle f_i,Tf_j\rangle,\qquad U_{ij}=\langle Tf_i,Tf_j\rangle,\qquad G=B^*B=\beta^2(U-W^2).\]

Haar integrals are evaluated by an exact 24-point quaternion cubature for the required moments, using integer array operations. This is an integration rule for continuous SU(2), not a replacement by a finite gauge group or a Monte Carlo estimate. Patch and cube matrices were recomputed with different maximal trees and matched exactly.

For a proved omitted-sector floor \(C\ge dI\), and \(E<d\), the Schur complement satisfies

\[S(E)=A-EI-B^*(C-EI)^{-1}B\ \ge\ F_d(E)=A-EI-\frac{G}{d-E}.\]

Exact inertia checks bound the two lowest full energies from below by \(L_0,L_1\); retained Ritz upper bounds are \(a_0^+,a_1^+\). Consequently,

\[L_1-a_0^+\ \le\ \Delta/c_K\ \le\ a_1^+-L_0.\]

Both energies are enclosed separately. The certificate is not obtained by subtracting two uncontrolled truncated eigenvalues.

Connection to BM dynamic closure

Keeping only a projected state generally loses autonomous evolution. Eliminating the omitted sector produces an initial-state term and a memory integral for returning excursions. Energy reduction also changes the norm through \(M(E)=-S'(E)=I+B^*(C-EI)^{-2}B\). This gives a concrete application of BM's requirement to retain the distinctions and response data needed for the declared task.

What the local estimate improves

Allocate a fraction of link electric energy to each plaquette, respecting the maximum plaquette incidence per link. Certified local square ground energies then give a stronger floor for \(QhQ\). The witness audit checks those auxiliary energy certificates too.

A separate short-loop counting argument yields, for the retained vacuum plus all distinct simple four- and six-cycles including the plaquette faces,

\[\|Q(-\beta T)\Pi\|^2\le\beta^2\min\{N_p^2,\ 3N_p+120z^2\}.\]

Here the graph is finite and simple with girth at least four, the plaquettes have distinct four-edge supports, \(N_p\) is their number, and each edge belongs to at most \(z\) plaquettes. This general bound concerns that specified short-loop retained space; completeness below an electric threshold is a separate requirement.

For bounded \(z\), the second estimate grows linearly with the number of plaquettes for the squared coupling. It still allows the coupling norm to grow as \(\sqrt{N_p}\), and the local energy floor retains an extensive vacuum-energy contribution. It is not a volume-independent gap bound.
