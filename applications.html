Worked directions

Applications

BM is most useful when a problem crosses representations and the correct distinctions change with the task, time horizon, receiver, or physical realization.

PDE closure

Compare state reductions that match current observables against reductions that remain closed under time evolution.

Inverse problems

Separate exact nullspace ambiguity from numerical instability and regularization-selected completions.

Control & response

Represent intervention channels, receiver obligations, feedback paths, and failure localization.

Quantum information

Distinguish algebraic recoverability from physically realizable reduced channels and task-specific correction.

Gauge theory

Track gauge-invariant carriers, loop observables, local/global noninheritance, and response/gap criteria.

Distributed systems

Expose communication, locality, causal cuts, memory, and gluing obligations that a local summary may omit.
