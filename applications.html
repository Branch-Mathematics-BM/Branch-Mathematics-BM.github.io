Applications

BM is most useful when a problem crosses representations and the correct distinctions change with the task, time horizon, receiver, or physical realization.

New finite demonstrations and application candidates

The 29 October survey supplies 24 candidates and four extensions. Nine exact analyzer contracts cover seven applications, with two additional enumeration checks. These are small mathematical demonstrations, not deployed product trials.
