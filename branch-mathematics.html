What is Branch Mathematics?

Branch Mathematics (BM) is a mathematical framework for carrying a mathematical problem through multiple distinct mathematical structures while preserving the laws, distinctions, and admissibility conditions belonging to each structure.

Its purpose is not to reduce all mathematics to a single representation. Instead, BM provides formal machinery for lawful representation, transformation, interaction, composition, reduction, realization, export, and re-entry across mathematical structures.

Foundational Principle

Many lawful mathematical branches

Different mathematical structures may participate in the same problem without losing their individual mathematical identities.

Lawful interaction

Interactions between branches must respect the mathematical laws and interface requirements governing the participating structures.

Combined computation

Information produced across branches may be combined when the required compatibility and admissibility conditions are satisfied.

Root or law result

A lawful interaction may produce a result that can participate in additional mathematical structure and further response.

Core Structures

Branch

A typed mathematical carrier of information, distinctions, structure, and lawful behavior.

Interaction

A lawful joint mathematical action or relationship between branches.

Leaf

Mathematical information extracted from an interaction.

Tree

An organized structure of branches and interactions that produces a mathematical response.

Root

A response capable of participating in further lawful mathematical structure or response.

Passport

A formal carrier of the distinctions, observations, compatibility conditions, and admissibility requirements needed across an interface.

Research Status

Branch Mathematics is an internally developed formal mathematical framework under continuing mathematical review, proof development, testing, comparison with existing mathematics, and application research.

Mathematical validity, external novelty, and independent external validation are treated as separate questions.
