Conditional

Yang–Mills block engine

For a fixed finite regulator, the vacuum-measure route uses the ground-state transform and conditional block kernels. Under a disjoint block partition and the required Poincaré/influence hypotheses, a local-to-global certificate has the form

\[\Delta_{a,L}\ge c(a)\lambda_{\rm loc}(a,L)(1-\theta_{a,L}).\]

The current sharper comparison-graph geometry uses an=10n²+2 and G(μ)=2e−μ(6+3e−μ+e−2μ)/(1−e−μ)³. In the present conditional certificate, the documented small-coupling check at |β|≤10−4 gives θQ<0.628 and an internal block lower bound above 1.8738. These are finite-regulator conditional estimates, not the continuum mass-gap theorem.

The unresolved step is regulator-uniform control along the continuum trajectory, including exclusion of collective soft modes and reconciliation of the relevant physical energy metric.
