Equations

Representative governing equations used across the current BM/GURF corpus.

Exact fibre factorization

\[R(x)=R(x')\Rightarrow T(x)=T(x')\iff \exists!D:R(X)\to W,\ D\circ R=T.\]

Dynamic Closure

\[R\circ f=\bar f\circ R\iff \ker R\subseteq\ker(R\circ f).\]

Completion fibre

\[\mathfrak C_A(\ell)=\bigcap_\alpha r_\alpha^{-1}(\ell_\alpha).\]

Approximate receiver width

\[\omega(R)=\sup_{R(x)=R(x')}\sup_i d_i(T_i(x),T_i(x')).\]

Unrestricted joint decoders satisfy the T47/T32 style bound \(\omega/2\le e^*\le\omega\) when the required representative conditions hold.

Steadman response efficiency

\[\eta_R=R_{\rm useful}/R_{\rm ideal}.\]

Finite-lattice Yang–Mills continuation

Here \(h=H/c_K\), \(A=\Pi h\Pi\), \(B=Qh\Pi\), \(C=QhQ\), and \(Q=I-\Pi\). Energies below are in units of \(c_K\).

Full declared Hamiltonian

\[h=K-\beta T,\qquad K=-\sum_e\Delta_e,\qquad T=\sum_p\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}U_p.\]

Exact sector elimination and norm

\[S(E)=A-EI-B^*(C-EI)^{-1}B,\qquad M(E)=-S'(E)=I+B^*(C-EI)^{-2}B.\]

The inverse must exist; the lower-bound calculation uses \(E<d\) when \(C\ge dI\).

Certificate from exact inertia and Ritz bounds

\[F_d(E)=A-EI-\frac{B^*B}{d-E},\qquad L_1-a_0^+\le\Delta/c_K\le a_1^+-L_0.\]

The negative inertia of \(F_d(L_j)\) must be at most \(j\), with \(L_j<d\); \(a_j^+\) bounds the corresponding retained Ritz energy from above.
