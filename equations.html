About the Registry

This page indexes mathematical expressions used in Branch Mathematics, GURF, and associated research.

Equations are recorded with their definitions, assumptions, mathematical domain, derivation status, dependencies, and limitations whenever applicable.

Branch Mathematics

BM Structural Principle

Status: Governing structural expression

\[ \text{lawful branches} \longrightarrow \text{lawful interaction} \longrightarrow \text{combined computation} \longrightarrow \text{Root or law result} \]

This expression summarizes the governing structural direction of Branch Mathematics. It is a structural schema rather than a numerical equation.

Equation Status

An equation may be classified as a definition, exact identity, derived relation, conditional relation, governing equation, approximation, proposed model, or conjectural expression.

Inclusion in this registry does not by itself establish novelty, physical realization, or independent external validation.
