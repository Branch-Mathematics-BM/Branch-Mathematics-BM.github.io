Roots and Rooting

Roots

Roots are receiver-relative, not universal final answers.

Root rule

A candidate export becomes a Root only when the receiving Passport is satisfied. The same object may be a Root for one receiver and fail for another.

\[\mathrm{Root}_\rho(E)\Longleftrightarrow E\in A_\rho\ \text{and all declared receiver obligations hold}.\]

Re-entry

Rooting reuses a Root in a downstream Branch. Rebranching begins a further BM development only if new Branch structure is actually introduced. Acceptance is not automatically transitive across a chain of receivers.

A Root accepted at time or receiver \(\rho_1\) can fail at \(\rho_2\) if \(\rho_2\) requires distinctions, regularity, provenance, or future closure not required earlier.
