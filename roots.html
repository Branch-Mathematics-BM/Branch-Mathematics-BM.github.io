Definition

Root

Active core structure

A Root is a mathematical response or resulting structure capable of participating in further lawful mathematical response.

A Root may therefore become input to additional Branches, Interactions, Trees, Operations, interfaces, or compatible mathematical constructions.

Role of a Root

Response Producing Further Response

A Root differs from a terminal result when the mathematical output remains structurally available for additional lawful development.

Structural Continuation

A Root may preserve enough mathematical structure to support continuation into another compatible mathematical operation or Branch.

Re-entry

A Root may re-enter a compatible mathematical structure when its type, Passport conditions, and admissibility requirements are satisfied.

Rooting

Interior Attachment

Status: Active research definition

Rooting studies the lawful attachment of a mathematical Branch, law family, or response structure to an interior point of another compatible mathematical structure.

The attachment may create a new location from which additional mathematical branching or response can occur.

Root Compatibility

A Root cannot be inserted arbitrarily into another mathematical structure.

Type Compatibility

The mathematical type of the Root must be accepted by the receiving structure or transformed lawfully into an accepted type.

Passport Compatibility

Required distinctions, constraints, invariants, and interface conditions must remain satisfied.

Law Compatibility

Root attachment does not override the laws governing either the originating or receiving mathematical structure.

Root Passport Transfer

Status: Active research structure

Information removed from one active representation may still be required by later mathematics.

When lawful compatibility permits, such information may persist as a typed carrier associated with a compatible Passport or Root.

The transferred information may instantiate a new Root Passport or join an existing compatible Passport structure.

Conservative Roots and Passports

Status: Candidate research structure

Some transformations may remove explicit representation of quantities that must nevertheless remain mathematically conserved.

Conservative Root and Passport structures investigate how required quantities such as energy, momentum, charge, invariants, or other conserved mathematical information can remain available across such transformations.

Root Registry

Formal Roots may receive stable identifiers within the Branch Mathematics Root registry.

Registry entries may record:

Root identifier · Mathematical type · Origin · Dependencies · Passport requirements · Attachment conditions · Conserved structure · Resulting Branches · Status · Revision

Research Status

Root theory remains under active formal development.

Every proposed Root construction must be checked against the mathematics of the participating structures. Root terminology does not itself prove that an attachment, continuation, conservation law, or physical realization is mathematically valid.
