About the Registry

This registry contains formal mathematical results used throughout Branch Mathematics, GURF, and associated research.

Each result should identify its assumptions, statement, proof status, dependencies, consequences, limitations, and current research status.

Result Classification

Theorem

A formally stated mathematical result supported by a complete proof under its stated assumptions.

Proposition

A proved mathematical result used as part of the broader framework or a larger theorem.

Lemma

A proved supporting result used in another mathematical argument.

Conditional Result

A result whose conclusion follows mathematically when explicitly stated assumptions or hypotheses hold.

Derived Identity

An exact mathematical relation obtained by lawful derivation from stated definitions or equations.

Conjecture

A mathematically stated claim that has not yet been proved.

Publication Standard

A result is not labeled a theorem merely because it appears plausible or performs well in examples. Formal theorem status requires a valid mathematical proof under explicitly stated assumptions.

Proof status, external novelty, physical interpretation, numerical evidence, and independent validation remain separate classifications.
