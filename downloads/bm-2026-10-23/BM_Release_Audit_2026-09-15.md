# Branch Mathematics release audit

15 September 2026. Applies to *Branch Mathematics: Foundations and Research Developments*, Research Edition dated 15 September 2026.

## Release decision

The scoped research edition is suitable for public technical review as a mathematical exposition with proofs, counterexamples, explicit status boundaries, references, and reproducible finite computations. It is not a certification that every historical BM research thread is complete, that every result is original, or that all proofs have been independently checked.

The recovered material supports a substantial book. It does not support the requested literal claim of “100%” completion of all BM. The full DD sequence through DD-361 remains unavailable as a proof source, some broader research families remain conditional or historical, and theorem-by-theorem external novelty review is unfinished. Those gaps are disclosed in the book rather than filled with invented statements.

## Governing baseline

The Version 1 architecture remains governed by the 12 September Edition 1.1 amendment. T01-T55 keep their stable meanings. The book adds later developments without silently changing the frozen foundation. BM remains above GURF. SSH remains retired; GRDE remains frozen at 008. Seed, Rooting, and Rebranching retain candidate status unless a particular native construction is specified.

The consolidated September 14 log governs W01-W35. Later numbers from the earlier scratchpad are retired scratch labels, not additional canonical theorems. FI01-FI42 are application and audit records with their source classifications and mandatory scope corrections. Listing them does not certify 42 new physical results.

## Repairs and strengthened statements

1. **Physical recovery has an explicit finite bound.** For a finite-dimensional task algebra and a channel with a chosen $r$-Kraus representation, the book proves the norm conversion giving $\delta_{\mathcal A}\le\min(2,2r\sqrt{\kappa_{\mathcal A}})$. It uses the established approximate OAQEC upper bound with attribution. It does not claim a sharp dimension-free bound for the original normalized operator-system defect.
2. **Approximate algebras have a counterexample.** Complete qubit dephasing approximately preserves two tilted task algebras whose generated algebra is not recoverable at the same tolerance. This supplies an actual no-greatest-element proof.
3. **Reduction order is checked.** Separately exactifying for two actions can produce different outputs in opposite orders. Alternation reaches the joint interior on a finite carrier; one pass needs a commutation justification.
4. **The spectral covering estimate uses a proved grid bound.** The book includes the $\sqrt n$ coordinate factor with ambient Hilbert-space centers. A more optimistic product formula is not certified without the additional covering theorem it would require. It is not falsely declared disproved.
5. **Monotonicity of a quantum failure model needs a channel relation.** A more damaged channel is treated as a postprocessing of the less damaged one. Set inclusion of named failures alone does not prove this relation.
6. **Admissibility is a subset condition.** The inclusion of lawful states into raw states is not replaced by an unjustified universal repair map. Descent through a redundancy quotient requires invariant tasks and compatible lawful states.
7. **Finite-width product bounds keep their sign assumptions.** Nonnegative initial data and coefficients, or an explicit absolute-sum bound, are supplied where required.
8. **No information-substance physics is inferred.** Injectivity preserves state distinctions. It does not prove universal physical injectivity, derive an energy functional, or create fields or particles.
9. **Current external physics status is attributed.** The book distinguishes an external September 2026 Navier-Stokes announcement from any BM result and from an independent verification of that announcement. The BM energy-only closure obstruction is proved directly on smooth solutions.

## Verification evidence

The retained Edition 1.1 programs were rerun and passed: 4,096 deterministic models, 648 partial-interaction models, 18,225 multiple-action models, 2,560 relational models, and 1,080 exact stochastic models. Exact rational quantum witnesses and 16 two-qubit matrix-unit constraints passed. Ninety-six modal heat quadratures agreed to below $2.7\times10^{-15}$ in the recorded normalization. Invariant-graph examples satisfied their stated bounds.

The new checks covered all 19,583 nested-partition and added-test comparisons through five states. There were no complementarity violations through four states and 90 at five. The incidence cycle identity passed all 18,616 five-state comparisons. W35 was checked on every column order through nine columns modulo reversal, matching the formulas for breadth, depth, and constrained depth. The six-column dynamic-program frontier agreed with independent exhaustive enumeration. Exact finite checks reproduced the reduction-order and quantum examples.

These are finite exhaustive, exact rational, and numerical checks. They are not formal proof certificates for the whole book. Written arguments supply the general statements and infinite-dimensional limits. Machine-readable outputs are included in the package.
