\[

\]

\[
x\sim_Fx' \iff F(x)=F(x')

\[
F(x)=F(x')\Longrightarrow T(x)=T(x'),

# Part B — Major Mathematical Corrections Produced by the Project

## 5. Serial-kernel correction

Early formulations could suggest that stage-local kernels automatically form a nested chain.

That is false when the stages live on different domains.

The corrected object is the equivalence relation induced by **partial compositions from a common original domain**.

**Status:** corrected and retained.

**Lesson:** information ordering must be typed relative to a common source state.

---

## 6. Constraint/provenance correction

Evidence restriction and admissibility restriction may both shrink a feasible set, but they are not the same provenance event.

Set intersections commute, so a universal mathematical claim of noncommutation was rejected.

The surviving distinction is semantic/provenance:

- new evidence changes what has been observed;
- a stronger admissibility assumption changes what is allowed to be true.

**Status:** original noncommutation theorem rejected; provenance distinction retained.

---

## 7. Approximate-signature correction

Threshold closeness is generally nontransitive.

Therefore approximate signatures cannot always be treated as equivalence classes or quotients.

The proper general object may be a:

- cover;
- hypergraph;
- tolerance relation;
- transition-compatible approximate relation.

**Status:** corrected.

This prevents arbitrary connected-component closure from creating chains whose total distortion greatly exceeds the declared threshold.

# Part D — Response and Distinguishability Algebra

## 14. Serial–Parallel Distinguishability Algebra

A distinct algebraic line existed from the early formal GURF work.

For

\[
F:X\to Y,\qquad G:X\to Z,
\]

parallel composition is

\[
F\parallel G=(F,G),
\]

with exact fibre refinement

\[
\boxed{
[x]_{F\parallel G}
=
[x]_F\cap[x]_G.
}
\]

At the kernel-pair level,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Eq}(F\parallel G)
=
\operatorname{Eq}(F)\cap\operatorname{Eq}(G).
}
\]

Parallel composition is associative, commutative up to coordinate swap, and idempotent up to distinguishability equivalence.

Serial deterministic post-processing

\[
T\circ F
\]

cannot refine exact source distinctions and preserves them exactly when \(T\) is injective on \(\operatorname{ran}F\).

### Permanent correction

Unrestricted serial–parallel distributivity is false.

A product-separable law survives:

\[
\boxed{
(T_Y\times T_Z)\circ(F\parallel G)
=
(T_Y\circ F)\parallel(T_Z\circ G).
}
\]

The branch also corrected an order-convention ambiguity: parallel observation is a join under the experiment-information order but a meet under the usual finer-below partition order.

**Status:** retained GURF-organized algebraic synthesis.  
**External novelty:** not established; strong relation/lattice algebra precedent.

\[
C:X\to Y
\]

\[

\]

\[

\boxed{

## 33. Writer-complete-cut no-go

If every future accessible response factors through a writer/carrier \(W\),

Globally,

The cut hypothesis is essential: a later independent writer that again touches the hidden state may reacquire the distinction.

## 35. Revelation–Retention–Recovery decomposition

Defect Calculus separates three failure mechanisms:

1. **unrevealed:** the lawful response history has not yet exposed a required distinction;
2. **retention/erasure:** the distinction appeared in history but the present carrier no longer preserves it;
3. **recovery/resource:** the distinction remains structurally available but the permitted reader cannot exploit it sufficiently.

For a decision-theoretic risk formulation, a candidate decomposition is

\[
R_{\mathcal R}(C_m)
=
R^*(H_m)
+
\left[
R^*(C_m)-R^*(H_m)
\right]
+
\left[
R_{\mathcal R}(C_m)-R^*(C_m)
\right].
\]

The three nonnegative terms correspond respectively to:

- unrevealed debt;
- retention debt;
- resource/recovery debt.

**Status:** active theorem candidate; requires reduction against statistical decision theory and data-processing/sufficiency results.

I_O(Q)\subseteq\mathcal D_O(F)
\]

for every such continuation.

A source-access writer \(W\) reduces the floor to

\[
I_O(Q,W)=
[\operatorname{Eq}(Q)\cap\operatorname{Eq}(W)]
\setminus\operatorname{Eq}(O).
\]

If \(W\) itself factors through \(Q\), the floor is unchanged; such a channel is computation, not reacquisition.

The global transport object becomes a directed path category. Invertible edges form the earlier defect-isomorphism subgroupoid; irreversible cuts are handled by reachability and confluence.

For terminating directed systems, standard Newman's lemma gives:

\[
\boxed{
\text{termination + local defect confluence}
\Rightarrow
\text{unique terminal defect normal form}.
}
\]

When confluence fails, route-robust analysis uses the join/meet envelope of reachable terminal defects.

**Status:** active theorem-development family.  
**Prior-art targets:** rewriting/confluence theory, closure operators, directed categories, abstract interpretation, irreversible resource theories.

## 70. Common-Writer Confluence Repair

For a finite family of terminal route carriers \(C_p\), define the harmful-pair graph \(G_p\) for each route and the union route graph

\[
G_{\rm conf}=\bigcup_pG_p.
\]

One common repair writer \(W:X\to\{1,\ldots,k\}\) makes every terminal route task-safe iff it properly colors \(G_{\rm conf}\). Hence

\[
\boxed{
k_{\min}^{\rm common}=\chi(G_{\rm conf}).
}
\]

If route identity is known before writer choice, route \(p\) needs only \(\chi(G_p)\) symbols. Therefore

\[
\chi(G_{\rm conf})\ge\max_p\chi(G_p),
\]

and the difference measures a common-writer/route-uncertainty premium.

This gives a finite exact realization of the earlier post-reveal-switching correction: pre-reveal common storage and route-specific post-reveal repair are different resource problems.

A common task-safe repair need not make the terminal carriers themselves identical; full carrier confluence is a stronger problem.

**Status:** active theorem-development family; graph-theoretic ingredients are standard.

## 71. Higher-Order Confluence Repair Spectrum

The confluence-repair programme now distinguishes raw exact-order defect from the monotone **through-order repair spectrum**

\[
\tau_{\le r}
=
\inf\left\{
m:\exists W\in\mathscr W_m,
\mathcal D_{p,O}^{[j]}(W)=\varnothing
\ \forall p,\ 0\le j\le r
\right\}.
\]

Because higher `r` adds constraints,

\[
\boxed{\tau_{\le r+1}\ge\tau_{\le r}.}
\]

For writer budget `m`, the maximum common adequacy order

\[
a_m=\sup\{r:\tau_{\le r}\le m\}
\]

satisfies the exact budget–order duality

\[
\boxed{\tau_{\le r}\le m\iff a_m\ge r.}
\]

A nonlinear dimension-floor theorem was added: if a local `d`-dimensional task quotient must be continuously and exactly recovered through `W:M->R^m`, then `m>=d`. Higher-order writers can therefore cure singular Jacobians but cannot defeat intrinsic topological task dimension.

A route-timing correction was also added:

\[
\operatorname{Eq}(P,W)\subseteq\operatorname{Eq}(O)
\]

is the route-aware contract, whereas

\[
\operatorname{Eq}(W)\subseteq\operatorname{Eq}(O)
\]

is the stronger route-blind contract. Hence

\[
\tau_{\le r}^{\rm blind}\ge\tau_{\le r}^{\rm aware}.
\]

For symmetry/gauge systems, unrestricted continuous invariant writers reduce to an embedding problem on the physical quotient:

\[
\boxed{
m_{\min}^{G,\mathrm{cont}}
=\operatorname{embdim}_{\mathbb R}(X/G).
}
\]

The cyclic benchmark `C_k` acting on `C` by `z->omega z` has complete invariant writer

\[
W_k(z)=z^k.
\]

It uses two real channels, has no nonconstant jet below order `k` at the fixed point, separates exactly the `C_k` orbits, and has radial inverse scaling `|z|=|W_k|^{1/k}`. Thus symmetry can force arbitrarily delayed physical revelation without increasing channel dimension, while worsening inverse regularity.

**Status:** active theorem-development family.  
**Prior-art targets:** jet theory, invariant theory, embedding theory, singularity theory, nonlinear observability, separating invariants.

## 72. Separating-Invariant Defect Algebra

For a lawful writer algebra `A` and task `O`, a finite writer family `F=(f_1,...,f_m)` is exact precisely when

\[
\bigcap_i\operatorname{Eq}(f_i)\subseteq\operatorname{Eq}(O).
\]

Define the minimum separating writer number

\[
s_{\mathcal A}(O)
=
\min\{m:\exists f_1,\ldots,f_m\in\mathcal A
\text{ task-separating}\}.
\]

For a degree-filtered polynomial writer algebra, the degree-constrained number `s_{<=d}(O)` is nonincreasing in `d`, and the minimum degree at channel budget `m`,

\[
d_m(O)=\min\{d:s_{\le d}(O)\le m\},
\]

obeys

\[
s_{\le d}(O)\le m\iff d_m(O)\le d.
\]

If `B=R[f_1,...,f_m]`, then its common indistinguishability relation equals the generator-map kernel relation. Every downstream writer in `B` factors through that generator map. Hence a harmful pair already merged by the generators cannot be repaired by further processing inside the same subalgebra; repair requires enlarging the writer algebra with a separating function. This is the algebraic closed-cut theorem.

For the cyclic action `C_k` on `C`, the two invariants `Re(z^k)` and `Im(z^k)` separate the orbits. The quotient is two-dimensional, so one continuous scalar invariant cannot suffice, while angular separation first requires degree `k`. Thus the structural channel-degree profile is `(2,k)`.

**Status:** active theorem-development family.  
**Prior-art targets:** separating invariants, invariant rings, finite generation, quotient embeddings, degree bounds, observable algebras.
