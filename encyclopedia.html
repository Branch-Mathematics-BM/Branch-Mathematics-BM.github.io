About the Encyclopedia

This encyclopedia is a formal reference for Branch Mathematics, GURF, and associated mathematical research.

Entries are organized by mathematical subject rather than by the chronological process through which the mathematics was developed.

Research status system: Established · Derived · Conditional · Experimental · Conjectural · Open

Branch Mathematics

Branch

A typed mathematical carrier of information, distinctions, structure, and lawful mathematical behavior.

Interaction

A lawful joint action or relationship between mathematical branches.

Leaf

Mathematical information extracted from an interaction between branches.

Tree

An organized structure of branches and interactions capable of producing a mathematical response.

Root

A mathematical response or structure capable of attaching to existing mathematics and generating further lawful response.

Passport

A formal specification of distinctions, observations, constraints, admissibility conditions, or information required across a mathematical interface.

GURF

GURF is a mathematical framework for lawful system responses.

It studies which distinctions in underlying states are required for a task and whether observation, compression, evolution, constraints, or interventions preserve enough information for future response.

Core Questions

What information must be preserved? What information may be removed? What prevents lawful continuation? What additional information repairs the obstruction?
