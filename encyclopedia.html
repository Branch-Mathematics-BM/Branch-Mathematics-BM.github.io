4. Branch Mathematics at a Glance

Branch Mathematics is organized around the principle that different mathematical structures may interact without being collapsed into one undifferentiated formalism.

\[ \boxed{ \text{lawful Branches} \longrightarrow \text{lawful Interaction} \longrightarrow \text{combined mathematical response} \longrightarrow \text{Root or law result} } \]

Each Branch retains its native laws.

BM adds explicit machinery for determining whether transformations, interfaces, quotienting, composition, and re-entry preserve what later mathematics requires.

5. GURF at a Glance

GURF becomes relevant when a mathematical problem contains a required response or task.

Let

\[ r: X \longrightarrow Y \]

be the retained representation and

\[ T: X \longrightarrow W \]

the required task.

Exact sufficiency means

\[ \boxed{ r(x)=r(x') \Longrightarrow T(x)=T(x'). } \]

Equivalently,

\[ T = \widetilde T \circ r. \]

7. Representation and Information

Observation

\[ r:X\to Y \]

Describes what information remains accessible.

Fibre

\[ r^{-1}(y) = \{x:r(x)=y\} \]

Every underlying state compatible with one representation value.

Task Sufficiency

\[ T = \widetilde T\circ r \]

The task can be recovered entirely from the retained information.

8. Completion Fibres

For a family of observations

\[ r_\alpha: X \longrightarrow Y_\alpha, \]

the compatible state set is

\[ \boxed{ \mathfrak C_A(\ell) = \bigcap_{\alpha\in A} r_\alpha^{-1}(\ell_\alpha). } \]

This construction appears throughout the GURF recovery and sufficiency program.

9. Structured Quotients

BM distinguishes ordinary observational equivalence from equivalence that is also compatible with mathematical operations.

For

\[ \sigma: X^n \longrightarrow X, \]

operation compatibility requires

\[ \boxed{ x_i\sim_P y_i \quad\forall i \Longrightarrow \sigma(x_1,\ldots,x_n) \sim_P \sigma(y_1,\ldots,y_n). } \]

The finitary algebraic result is recorded as BM-SQ-01.

10. Interfaces and Coherence

One exact interface condition is

\[ \boxed{ P_2\circ F = G\circ P_1. } \]

The broader coherence program includes:

parallel paths · equalizers · pullbacks · coequalizers · gluing · path closure · naturality · approximate coherence · higher coherence

11. Obstruction and Repair

GURF identifies a task obstruction when

\[ \boxed{ r(x)=r(x') \quad\text{but}\quad T(x)\neq T(x'). } \]

Repair adds information \(q\) so that the combined observation

\[ (r,q): X \longrightarrow Y\times Z \]

becomes sufficient for the task.

12. Dynamic Continuation

If evolution is

\[ \Phi_t: X \longrightarrow X, \]

then the present representation remains sufficient for a future task when

\[ \boxed{ T\circ\Phi_t = \widetilde T_t\circ r. } \]

A distinction irrelevant now may become essential later.

17. Navier–Stokes Status

Status: active derived Leaves and theorem-kernel mathematics.

The project contains derived structures involving derivative covariance, strain-gradient information, helical decomposition, triad interactions, loading, redistribution, and related continuation quantities.

These results are not currently presented as a proof of global regularity or as a Clay Millennium solution.

18. Yang–Mills Status

Status: active conditional research.

Current mathematics includes boundary susceptibility, first-exit leakage, coherent return, shell-depth recursion, local thresholds, Gauss-law support, and global-return obstruction.

The four-dimensional Yang–Mills existence and mass-gap problem remains open.

19. Gravity Status

Status: active mathematical-physics investigation.

General Relativity remains an essential comparison theory.

Alternative response or information-geometric descriptions require independent derivation, physical consistency, observable consequences, and falsifiable comparison.

20. Quantum Status

Status: active mathematical and interpretive investigation.

Entanglement is treated through joint state structure, measurable statistics, distinguishability, and information availability.

No controllable superluminal communication mechanism is assumed.

26. Worked Mathematics

Worked examples are used to demonstrate the behavior of formal definitions without silently promoting the examples into universal laws.

For example,

\[ R_{n+1} = \frac12R_n+1 \]

is used to demonstrate a repeated-Root dynamical system.

Its fixed point is

\[ R_*=2, \]

and for \(R_0=0\),

\[ R_n = 2(1-2^{-n}). \]

The Equation Registry includes the corresponding quantitative convergence figure.

28. Relationship Between Proof and Framework

BM/GURF terminology does not turn a statement into a theorem.

Mathematical validity comes from definitions, assumptions, and valid derivation.

Framework placement determines how a mathematical result is used, not whether it is true.

29. Internal Status and External Status

The encyclopedia distinguishes three separate questions.

Question Current meaning
Internal formal status whether the structure is accepted within the current research architecture
External novelty or priority whether equivalent mathematical structure is already known elsewhere
Independent validation whether the work has been reproduced, reviewed, or accepted outside the internal development process

These questions are not interchangeable.
