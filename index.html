Branch Mathematics

Branch Mathematics is a mathematical metaframework for determining which distinctions a problem must preserve across models, dynamics, observations, interfaces, and tasks—and what happens when those distinctions are compressed, transformed, lost, recovered, or made unnecessary.

The central idea

A representation is judged by what it must still do.

Mathematical models preserve some distinctions and discard others. Branch Mathematics asks whether the discarded distinctions are genuinely unnecessary—or whether dynamics, another subsystem, a receiver, or a later task still needs them.
