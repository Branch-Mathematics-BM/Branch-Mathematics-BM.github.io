What BM is for

A representation can answer a question now and still fail tomorrow, fail after a legal interaction, fail under a stronger receiver, or fail when two separately valid pieces must coexist. BM makes those failure points explicit instead of hiding them inside notation.

Task sufficiency

Does the representation identify only states that every declared task may safely identify?

Dynamic closure

Does the reduced state evolve autonomously under the declared dynamics, including domain availability?

Joint realization

Can locally valid Branch results coexist in one lawful global realization?
