Welcome

This site is the public research home of Branch Mathematics and related mathematical work.

The encyclopedia presents definitions, mathematical structures, equations, theorems, derivations, applications, research questions, revisions, and open problems.

Mathematical claims are separated by status, including established results, derived results, conditional results, experimental research, and open questions.

Branch Mathematics

Branch Mathematics (BM) is meta-structural mathematics for lawful representation and transformation.

BM studies mathematical branches, interactions, roots, passports, leaves, trees, operations, configurations, interfaces, composition, realization, and re-entry while preserving the mathematical laws governing each structure.

GURF

GURF is a mathematical framework for lawful system responses.

It studies which distinctions in underlying states are required for particular tasks and when observation, compression, constraints, evolution, or intervention remove information required for future responses.

About This Project

Branch Mathematics is maintained as an evolving mathematical research corpus.

Revisions are recorded through mathematical versions and research changelogs so that definitions, equations, and results can be corrected, generalized, or replaced as the work develops.
