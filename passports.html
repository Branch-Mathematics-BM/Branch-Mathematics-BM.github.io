Passports

A Passport is a contract for lawful transfer and reuse. It says exactly what the receiver requires—not what the sender hopes is sufficient.

Passport fields

Type

Required native object kind and codomain.

Domain

Where the transfer is defined and which availability distinctions must be retained.

Tasks

Observations or downstream operations whose values must remain recoverable.

Laws / context

Regime, conservation, gauge, positivity, locality, boundary, or other native obligations.

Continuation

Future-step, memory, path, or feedback obligations if the output will evolve or re-enter the system.

Tolerance

Exact or approximate acceptance metric and threshold.

Passport sufficiency: observational fields reduce to exact factorization tests when representable as functions, but a full Passport may contain native obligations that are not captured by one observation map.
