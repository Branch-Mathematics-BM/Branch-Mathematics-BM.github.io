Structural rule
  • COMPUTABLE: parsed native mathematics the CAS can operate on.
  • SYMBOLIC: parsed mathematics containing an undefined function or map.
  • CONSTRAINT: a recognized constraint that is not yet solved by this interface.
  • FORMAL / UNRESOLVED: mathematical notation whose semantics are preserved rather than guessed.

Only parsed computable incidence is allowed to produce a verified computational Branch. Symbolic/constraint statements remain parsed unresolved obligations. Formal notation may be grouped into a provisional formal cluster, but that cluster is not asserted to be a BM Branch.
