Syntax and interpretation
  • Use ^ for powers and * for multiplication: x^2, m*a. Common Unicode powers and ×, ÷, − are accepted.
  • Examples: factor(x^2-1); diff(sin(x),x); integrate(x^2,x); sqrt(2).
  • Trigonometric arguments are in radians. Symbols have no automatic physical units. Complex answers may appear.
  • Algebraic transformations apply where the original expressions are defined. Domain restrictions are not automatically reconstructed after cancellation.
  • These are computer-algebra results, not a general theorem prover. Unsupported PDEs, physical assumptions, and unresolved equations are identified explicitly.

What runs in the background?

The calculator parses your input, identifies equations and unknowns, solves the supported mathematical problem, and reports the method and remaining limitations. The advanced BM checker tests task sufficiency, dynamic closure, and exact finite-state refinements. Standard symbolic mathematics is powered by Nerdamer 2.0.0; it is not presented as a new BM theorem.
